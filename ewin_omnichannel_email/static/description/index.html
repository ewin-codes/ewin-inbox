EWIN Inbox: Email

Email connector for EWIN Inbox. A mailbox becomes a two-way channel: incoming mail opens conversations, replies go out over SMTP.

Odoo 18LGPL-3FreeTested on a live account

Highlights

  • IMAP polling for incoming mail, SMTP for replies
  • Threading by In-Reply-To and References
  • TLS enforced on both IMAP and SMTP
  • Attachments in both directions
  • Senders matched to or created as Odoo contacts

Requirements

  • EWIN Inbox (ewin_omnichannel_core) installed
  • IMAP credentials for the mailbox
  • SMTP credentials for sending

Data and external services

Mail is read from and sent through the IMAP and SMTP servers you configure. No other service is involved.
