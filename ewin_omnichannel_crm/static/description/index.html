EWIN Inbox: CRM

Links EWIN Inbox conversations to CRM leads and copies what the customer wrote into the lead's chatter, live.

Odoo 18LGPL-3Free

Highlights

  • A conversation carries a lead; one lead carries as many conversations as the customer starts
  • Every message of a linked conversation lands in the lead's chatter as an internal note, with its time and attachments
  • Create Lead opens a lead prefilled from what the channel knows about the customer, already linked
  • A Conversations button and Link Conversation on the lead
  • Unlinking removes exactly the copies the link created

Requirements

  • EWIN Inbox (ewin_omnichannel_core) installed
  • Odoo CRM (crm) installed

Data and external services

This module talks to no external service; it only moves records inside Odoo.
