EWIN Inbox: CRM

CRM bridge for EWIN Inbox. Links Inbox conversations to CRM leads and mirrors what the customer wrote into the lead's own chatter, live, so a salesperson reading a lead sees the whole exchange without opening the Inbox.

Odoo 18 LGPL-3 Active Development

Highlights

  • A conversation carries a Lead; one lead carries as many conversations as the customer starts
  • Every message of a linked conversation is copied into the lead's chatter as an internal note, live, with its own timestamp and attachments
  • Create Lead opens a lead prefilled from what the channel knows about the customer, already linked
  • Conversations smart button and Link Conversation on the lead attach and count what is linked
  • Unlinking or re-pointing a lead removes exactly the copies that link created

Requirements

  • EWIN Inbox (ewin_omnichannel_core) installed
  • Odoo CRM (crm) installed
