EWIN Inbox

Omnichannel customer inbox for Odoo 18. Answer Telegram, WhatsApp, Email, Instagram, Messenger, Viber and Live Chat from one Odoo-native screen, linked to your contacts and mirrored into Discuss.

Every channel in one conversation list, with operator, team, state and priority.

What it does

  • One inbox for Telegram, WhatsApp, Email, Instagram, Messenger, Viber and Odoo Live Chat
  • Two-way text and media, with delivery and read statuses where the channel reports them
  • Customers matched to Odoo contacts; with EWIN Inbox: CRM, conversations attach to leads
  • Operator and team assignment; replying takes an unassigned conversation
  • Internal notes that never leave Odoo
  • Canned replies and scheduled replies
  • Close reasons, optional or mandatory, from a configurable list
  • Realtime list and thread updates, unread counters on the menu
  • Every conversation mirrored into Odoo Discuss; a reply typed there reaches the customer too
  • Retry queue for failed sends, error log, conversation and message reporting

Requirements

  • Odoo 18 Community or Enterprise
  • A public HTTPS address for the webhook-based connectors
  • Provider accounts and API credentials for the channels you connect

Data and external services

The core module talks to no external service. Messages leave Odoo only through the connector modules you install, each to its own provider; their pages say exactly where.
