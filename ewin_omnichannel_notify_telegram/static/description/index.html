EWIN Inbox: Telegram Notifications

Notifies your team in Telegram the moment a customer writes to EWIN Inbox, on any channel. A second, dedicated bot for staff — separate from the customer-facing Telegram connector — so nobody has to keep Odoo open to know a reply is waiting.

Odoo 18 LGPL-3 Active Development

Highlights

  • Notifies the assigned operator personally once they've connected their own Telegram, or the shared team chat when there's nobody to notify personally yet
  • Throttled per conversation (10 minutes by default) so a burst of messages produces one notification, not one per message — the first message always gets through
  • Each notification names the channel and the customer, previews what they wrote, and links straight back into the conversation in Odoo
  • One-time, expiring Telegram links for operators to connect their own account from Preferences — no shared credentials, no admin typing chat ids by hand
  • A separate bot and webhook from the customer-facing Telegram connector, and no dependency on it — install this even where that connector isn't

Requirements

  • EWIN Inbox (ewin_omnichannel_core) installed
  • A second Telegram bot token from @BotFather, distinct from any customer-facing bot
  • A public HTTPS endpoint reachable by Telegram for the webhook
